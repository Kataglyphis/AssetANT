Changes (CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)), in two steps.

1. AssetANT release Colosseum-gltf-v1, Colosseum-gltf.tar.xz, SHA-256
   172fed54c2865711c59b5abe2622aa6db9dc2429ae03b58e71ff7ca1ca33c6fa: converted from Blender 5.3 to
   glTF 2.0 by AssetANT's scripts/blender/export_gltf.py with assets/Colosseum-gltf/export-gltf.json.
   - Box-projected image materials and the procedural masonry are baked into tiling textures (base
     colour, roughness, metallic, and a normal map from the bump relief) on generated UVs; the wall
     dirt is left out.
   - Materials made only of 3D noise are reduced to their mean colour.
   - The crowd's per-face colours are quantised to 32 materials.
   - N-gons are triangulated, and the hidden older crowd is left out.
   export-report.json records the Blender version, the source file's SHA-256 and each material's
   treatment.

2. This asset: each of Colosseum-gltf's 69 PNG and JPEG textures re-encoded as one KTX2 file by
   AssetANT's scripts/ktx2/gltf_to_ktx2.py with assets/Colosseum-ktx2/ktx2.json and KTX-Software
   4.4.2's ktx create.
   - UASTC 4x4 blocks, a lossy encoding, at quality level 2 without rate-distortion optimisation,
     each level supercompressed with Zstandard at level 18.
   - A full mip chain below the source image, box-filtered with wrapping edges.
   - Base colour maps are sRGB, normal and metallic-roughness maps linear data.
   - The glTF names every texture through KHR_texture_basisu alone and requires that extension; no
     PNG or JPEG copy is left. Colosseum-ktx2.bin is Colosseum-gltf.bin byte for byte, and the
     meshes, materials, samplers, nodes, cameras and light are unchanged.
   ktx2-report.json records the encoder and its SHA-256, the settings, and each texture's source
   file, colour space, hashes and base-level PSNR against its source.
