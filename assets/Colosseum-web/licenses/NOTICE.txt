"Colosseum" by Sulo456 (https://github.com/Sulo456): the Roman Colosseum with arena show, crowd and
surroundings, built in Blender with MaterialCraft materials.
Original: AssetANT release Colosseum-v1, Colosseum.tar.xz,
SHA-256 29c155ef276ce1f86d84507c611ca9e3144d4e5443a638f451dec321b1915fac.

The complete official license text is in CC-BY-4.0.txt, the same file as in this repository's
JurassicPark asset (SHA-256 9e5f1b3c610b9c2da5c313bf81d577a7d1acec686bdb0384edefa6df0f90cd94).

Changes (CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(1)(B)): converted from Blender 5.3 to one binary glTF 2.0 file
for web browsers by AssetANT's scripts/blender/export_gltf.py with
assets/Colosseum-web/export-gltf.json.
- Box-projected image materials and the procedural masonry are baked into tiling textures of at
  most 512 pixels (base colour, roughness, metallic, and a normal map from the bump relief) on
  generated UVs; the wall dirt is left out.
- Materials made only of 3D noise are reduced to their mean colour.
- The crowd is decimated to 6 % of its triangles and its per-face colours are quantised to 16
  materials; every other mesh above 20,000 triangles is decimated to 35 %.
- N-gons are triangulated, the hidden older crowd is left out, and no tangents are stored.
export-report.json records the Blender version, the source file's SHA-256 and each material's
treatment.
